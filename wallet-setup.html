Use the Nomad Coin Wallet on Your Device

Your wallet lives on the web at wallet.jeremyahenry.com; these simple steps just make it feel like a native app on your device.

Free, fast, and no app store needed.

Quick guide by device

📱 Android 🍎 iPhone or iPad 💻 Chromebook 🖥️ Windows, Mac, or Linux

1. Android Phone or Tablet

  • Open Chrome and go to wallet.jeremyahenry.com.
  • Tap the menu icon (three dots ⋮ in the top right corner).
  • Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  • Confirm. The Nomad Coin wallet icon will appear on your home screen and run full-screen like a standalone app.
  • Note: If the site references an official downloadable APK wrapper, you can also sideload that directly.

2. iPhone or iPad

  • Open Safari and go to wallet.jeremyahenry.com.
  • Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing upward at the bottom or top of the screen).
  • Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  • Tap Add. It will appear on your home screen and launch as a clean, full-screen web app.

3. Chromebook

  • Open Chrome and go to wallet.jeremyahenry.com.
  • Click the install icon (monitor with an arrow or plus) in the right side of the address bar, or click the 3-dots menu and select "Install Nomad Coin..." or "Add to shelf".
  • Confirm to install it as a progressive web app (PWA).

4. Windows, Mac, or Linux Desktop

  • Open Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge and navigate to wallet.jeremyahenry.com.
  • Click the Install icon in the right side of the address bar, or open browser menu and select "Install Nomad Coin...".
  • The wallet will open in its own dedicated window and pin to your taskbar or dock.
